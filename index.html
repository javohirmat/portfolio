Projects

  • Tomaris

    What I'm building: an on-prem gateway for Uzbek banks that masks card numbers, PINFL and phone numbers before prompts reach AI models, then restores them in the reply. A pilot with Kapitalbank is planned; the demo on the site uses scripted replies.

  • uzmorph

    An improved Uzbek morphological analyzer built on Apertium (v0.9.0, GPL-3.0). It gets 93.5% lemma+features accuracy on 1,731 held-out tokens, versus 87.8% for ZirhUzMorph; the eval labels are mostly model-built, with limited human review. Along the way I reported 23 bugs to upstream apertium-uzb.

  • UzGenBench

    An open-ended Uzbek benchmark. Instead of picking from options, the model has to write the answer, across 162 code, 74 math and logic, and 100 safety items. Access is gated; the safety answer keys are unreviewed drafts.

  • Uzbek Legal Corpus

    25 cleaned, audited texts of Uzbekistan's Constitution, codes and major laws from lex.uz, split into 7,368 articles as JSONL. Public domain (CC0), for research and model training.

  • UzBLiMP in progress

    A benchmark of Uzbek grammatical minimal pairs, built with a collaborator, to test whether models know Uzbek grammar.
