Projects

  • Tomaris

    An AI assistant for banks, trained for Uzbek, that answers only from documents the team has approved and hands the chat to a human operator when it can't. Built because banks here run their AI on foreign APIs; Tomaris can be deployed inside their own infrastructure.

  • uzmorph

    An improved Uzbek morphological analyzer built on Apertium (v0.9.0, GPL-3.0). It scores 93.5% on its test set versus 87.8% for ZirhUzMorph; along the way I reported 23 bugs to upstream apertium-uzb.

  • UzGenBench

    An open-ended Uzbek benchmark where the model has to write the answer: 162 code, 74 math and logic, and 100 safety items. Made because most Uzbek evaluations are multiple choice.

  • Uzbek Legal Corpus

    25 cleaned, audited texts of Uzbekistan's Constitution, codes and major laws from lex.uz, split into 7,368 articles as JSONL. Public domain (CC0), for research and model training.

  • UzBLiMP in progress

    A benchmark of Uzbek grammatical minimal pairs, built with a collaborator, to test whether models know Uzbek grammar.
