Projects

  • Tomaris

    I'm building an on-prem gateway for Uzbek banks that masks card numbers, PINFL and phone numbers before prompts reach AI models, then restores them in the reply.

  • uzmorph

    An improved Uzbek morphological analyzer built on Apertium (v0.9.0, GPL-3.0). It gets 93.5% lemma+features accuracy on 1,731 held-out tokens, versus 87.8% for ZirhUzMorph; the eval labels are mostly model-built, with limited human review. Along the way I reported 23 bugs to upstream apertium-uzb. Also on Hugging Face.

  • UzGenBench

    An open-ended Uzbek benchmark. Instead of picking from options, the model has to write the answer, across 162 code, 74 math and logic, and 100 safety items. Access is gated; the safety answer keys are unreviewed drafts.

  • Uzbek Legal Corpus

    25 cleaned, audited texts of Uzbekistan's Constitution, codes and major laws from lex.uz, split into 7,368 articles as JSONL. Public domain (CC0), for research and model training.

  • WeirdChat study ongoing research

    An independent study of whether Qwen3.6-27B fabricates having executed code. With thinking off, 7 of 32 answers (21.9%) made that claim, hand-scored. Cleaned prompts and responses are on GitHub.

  • UzBLiMP in progress

    A benchmark of Uzbek grammatical minimal pairs, built with a collaborator, to test whether models know Uzbek grammar.

  • Poverty, income inequality, trade and migration in Uzbekistan and Pakistan

    First author of five. Published in IJSSER, Vol. 10, Issue 12, December 2025. Compares poverty, trade and remittances in Uzbekistan and Pakistan, finding 14.5% of Uzbekistan's population below $3.65 a day versus 4.9% in Pakistan.

  • The measures to encourage WIUT students to replace cars with bicycles

    Survey of 107 Westminster International University in Tashkent students. Main benefit found was physical and mental health; main barrier was road safety. One of five winners from over 800 submissions in the ALT FL Journal research paper competition (July 2025).
